# Fuse gives a Card that is not Discovered

Before, Heynbord had no Fuse. The rules glossary said that "Fuse" in the original games was a random recipe system, and that **Combine** and **Craft** replace it. Now the Workshop has a fourth action, **Fuse**. It changes 2 to 4 Card copies and Essence into one copy of a Card that the Player has not Discovered. Craft gives only a Discovered Card, so Fuse is the only Workshop path to a new Card. It also gives a use to spare copies other than Extract (Pillar 4).

The rules of a Fuse:

- All input copies have the same **Rank**, and they are all Creature Cards or all Skill Cards.
- Common and Uncommon need 2 copies, Rare needs 3 and Epic needs 4. A Legendary copy is never an input, and a Specialist is never a result.
- The new Card has a Base Rank that is the Rank of the inputs, and the Player gets it in its Base Rank.
- Each input copy gives its Race (or Class) one equal share of the chance. For example, Human + Human + Elf gives Human 2 in 3 and Elf 1 in 3. A Race or Class with no Card that is not Discovered has no share. Then one Card of that Race or Class is selected at random.
- When no Card can be given, the Player cannot Fuse.
- It costs Essence, and it unlocks at Player level 6, as Craft.

## Considered Options

- **The inputs share a Base Rank, not a copy Rank.** Rejected. Each Class has only 1 Epic Skill Card, and each Race has only 2 Epic Creature Cards. Four Epic Warrior inputs would be 4 copies of Onslaught, so Onslaught is already Discovered and the Fuse can give nothing. With the copy Rank, a Common Card that the Player Combined to Rare is a Rare input, and each recipe can occur. This also agrees with the glossary: a Rank is the grade of a copy.
- **"New" means "0 copies now", not "not Discovered".** Rejected. Then a Player can Extract the last copy of a Card and Fuse to get it back, and Fuse becomes a second Craft with luck. Craft is the path to the Card that the Player selects. Fuse is the path to Discovery. The cost: when the Player has Discovered all Cards of a Rank and a Race or Class, that Fuse is not available.
- **When the Fuse pool is empty, Fuse gives any Card at random** (as New Card First in a Pack). Rejected. The Player would give copies and Essence and could get a Card that they already own. Pillar 1 says that the Player never loses progress because of luck. A Fuse that always gives a new Card passes the Pillar 1 test, the same as a Pack that costs only currency from play.

## Consequences

- The Economy milestone "All cards Discovered" can move earlier, so the economy sim must use Fuse and check the pacing targets again.
- The Fuse panel shows the chance of each Race or Class and the Cards in the Fuse pool, because the game shows all drop chances (Pillar 1).
