# v1 has 90 Creature Cards

Heynbord v1 has 15 Creature Cards for each of its 6 Races: Human, Elf, Undead, Orc, Goblin and Feral. Each Race has 5 Common, 5 Uncommon, 3 Rare and 2 Epic. This gives 90 Creature Cards and 118 collectible Cards with the planned 28 Skill Cards. An exact set of the same size for each Race gives each Race equal coverage, and it keeps the balance checks the same for all Races. There are no new Classes for Goblin and Feral, so a Class can be the main Class of more than one Race.

The 2 Epic Cards of each Race are one named champion with Unique and one archetypal powerhouse. Legendary remains a Rank that the Player reaches through Combine, not a Base Rank.

Feral is the one Race with no people. It is the host of wild creatures that serve no people. A creature that fights for a people stays a card of that people's Race.

## Considered Options

- **18 Creature Cards for each Race.** Rejected. It adds art and balance work for one developer, and 15 cards are enough for distinct Race identities and Deck choices.
- **4 Races with 60 Creature Cards, and Goblin and Feral after v1.** Rejected. This was the v1 plan before. Two more battle plans give more Deck choices at release than more cards in the 4 old Races. We accepted more art and balance work for it.
- **Smaller Goblin and Feral Races.** Rejected. Equal Races keep the Race shape and the balance checks the same for all Races.
- **A people of big brutes (ogres, trolls, giants) in place of Feral.** Rejected. A host of wild creatures gives a more distinct theme.
