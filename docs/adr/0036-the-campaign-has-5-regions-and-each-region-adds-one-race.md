# The Campaign has 5 Regions, and each Region adds one Race

The GDD had 3 Regions: Hearthvale (Human and Orc), the Thornwood (Elf and Feral) and the Hollow Marches (Undead and Goblin). Thus the Player met the Goblins only in Region 3, and the Races of each Region were pairs with no order between them. We decided that the Campaign has **5 Regions** of 10 Stages each in v1, so 50 Stages. Each Region keeps the newest Race of the Region before it and adds one new Race, its **Debut Race**: Human and Orc (Hearthvale), Orc and Goblin (the Red Mesas), Goblin and Elf (the Glowcap Hollows), Elf and Feral (the Thornwood), Feral and Undead (the Hollow Marches). The Boss of a Region is of a Debut Race of that Region. A Debut Race is only about the enemies: Packs, Race Packs and Craft do not change, and the Player can get cards of each Race at any time.

We did this because a chain teaches one new Race at a time, and each Region still shows a Race that the Player knows. Each Race gets its own Region in which it is new, so its Keywords (for example Sabotage and Hobble for the Goblins) can each get a Stage of their own.

## Considered Options

- **3 Regions, with a Region 3 of Elf, Undead and Feral.** Rejected: 3 Races in 10 Stages give less focus, and each Race gets fewer Stages to teach its Keywords.
- **v1 with Regions 1 to 3 only, and Regions 4 and 5 in v1.x.** Rejected: the Feral and Undead cards are then in no Stage card pool, and not in the Heynspire pool, which comes from the Campaign card pool.
- **5 Regions of 6 Stages.** Rejected: 6 Stages are too few for one new thing in each Stage, then a final test and a Boss Stage.

## Consequences

- The Campaign has 50 Stages. M3 has 40 new Stages, and that is a schedule risk. If M3 is late, apply the cut rule of the Roadmap.
- The XP table (`PLAYER_LEVEL_XP`) does not change. The Coin and XP for each win continue for Regions 4 and 5 (Economy 2.1).
- Heynspire unlocks after the Region 5 Boss Stage, at about Player level 23 on the First-try Path (it was level 15 after Region 3). Its Floors start at the strength of the Region 5 Boss.
- The Star chests of 5 Regions give 750 Heynstones (it was 450), so the v1 Heynstone supply is about 2,000.
- The art needs 5 Region Maps and 5 Battle Paintings. Until a Region has its Battle Painting, it uses the Hearthvale painting.
- Dungeon 3 uses 3 of the 5 Campaign Bosses: Baron Brassbelly, the Old Bramble and Queen Marrow.
