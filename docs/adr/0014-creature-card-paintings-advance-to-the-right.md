# Creature Card paintings advance to the right

A Creature Card has one painting. The player Side stands on the left of the Board, and the enemy Unit is a horizontal mirror of that painting. The painting therefore advances to the right of the image, in a three-quarter view, so each Side meets the other. The face and the weapon may turn, so a Pivot figure can look back, and the chest still points right. A fortification shows its blocking face to the right. We keep the light from the upper left, so we repaint a figure that advances left instead of flipping the file. We paint no charge at the viewer, because a mirror of that charge still runs at the viewer. Skill Cards have no Unit, so this rule does not cover them. Existing paintings that advance left stay until a later art pass. Militia Recruit is the first repaint.
