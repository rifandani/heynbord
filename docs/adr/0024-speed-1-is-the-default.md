# Speed 1 is the default, and the Role sets more Speed

No rule told when a Unit has Speed 2. Of the 71 Creature Cards that can move, 50 had Speed 1 and 19 had Speed 2. Among the 19 were a Frontliner (Militia Recruit), although GDD 5.6 says "Frontliner: low Speed", and 3 Goblin Strikers, although Goblin is not a fast Race. Each Square of Speed costs 2 Power Points, the same as 1 Attack. Thus a card designer could buy Speed for any card, and the Player could not read the Speed from the Role.

We decided:

- **The default Speed is 1.** This includes each Frontliner, Shooter and Support, and each Striker that is not an Orc Striker.
- **Only a Runner has Speed 2 or more.** A Flying Runner also has Speed 2 or more, because a Speed 1 Unit needs about 9 Turns to reach the enemy Hero.
- **An Orc melee Striker can have Speed 2.** "Fast" is part of the Orc identity (GDD 3.2). Another Race that wants a fast Striker uses Charge N, which gives the Speed only in the Turn of the summon.
- **Only a ground Runner can have Speed 3 or 4, and each Race has at most 1 such card.** A Unit that has both Flying and Speed 3 is too hard to stop before it reaches the Hero.
- **The rule is a guideline.** A card can break it only with a written reason in [10 — Card Concepts](../game/10-card-concepts.md), as a card outside its Power Budget can. A content test checks only the hard limits: a Wall has Speed 0, a Ranged Unit has Speed 1 or less, and no Unit has Speed above 4.

Militia Recruit, Sidestep Shiv, Mine Sapper and Boss Snikkit go to Speed 1. Each card keeps its old power within 1 point, with more HP or Attack.

## Considered Options

- **The default Speed is 2.** Rejected. Battles become faster, and Ranged Units get fewer free attacks. But all 50 Speed 1 cards need new stats, the Power Budget changes, and all Matchup and Stage results become old.
- **The Role alone sets the Speed, with no Race exception.** Rejected. The 3 Orc Strikers with Speed 2 fit the Orc identity, and Charge N alone does not make a Unit fast after its first Turn.
- **No rule: each card buys Speed with Power Points.** Rejected. This is how the data got a Speed 2 Frontliner. The Player cannot read the Speed from the Role.
- **A hard rule with a test for each Role.** Rejected. A fast champion card is sometimes good design. A written reason is sufficient, as for the Power Budget.

## Consequences

- The main pair moves little: Vanguard wins 47.2% against Raiders (it was 47.6%, 1000 seeds).
- The diagnostic Decks with the changed cards are weaker. Tunnel Rats wins 37.5% against Vanguard (it was 42.5%), and Human Light wins 39.6% (it was 46.7%). Militia Recruit is at 16 of its budget of 15, so it has no more room.
- Stages 1-1, 1-4 and 1-5 got new enemy Decks or Ranks ([14 — Campaign Stages](../game/14-campaign-stages.md)).
- Knockback 2 and 3 can lock a Unit whose Speed is lower than N ([08 — Archetypes](../game/08-archetypes.md)). Now that Speed 1 is the default, this lock can hit most Units. Only Combine makes these copies, and the lock is still for review.
