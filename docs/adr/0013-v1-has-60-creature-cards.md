# v1 has 60 Creature Cards

Heynbord v1 has 15 Creature Cards for each of its 4 Races: 5 Common, 5 Uncommon, 3 Rare and 2 Epic. This gives 60 Creature Cards and 88 collectible Cards with the planned 28 Skill Cards. We reduced the earlier target of 18 Creature Cards for each Race because an exact, smaller set gives each Race equal coverage, reduces art and balance work for one developer, and keeps enough Cards for distinct Race identities and Deck choices.

The 2 Epic Cards of each Race are one named champion with Unique and one archetypal powerhouse. Legendary remains a Rank that the Player reaches through Combine, not a Base Rank.
