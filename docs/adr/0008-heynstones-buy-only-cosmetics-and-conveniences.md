# Heynstones buy only Cosmetics and Conveniences

Heynbord has two currencies. **Coin** (shown as Gold, Silver and Copper) comes from play and pays for all power: Packs, Combine and Gear upgrades. **Heynstones** are rare. In v1 the Player earns them only from one-time sources. From v2 the Player can also buy them with real money. Heynstones buy only Cosmetics and Conveniences in the Bazaar. A Convenience changes comfort or organization, for example an extra Deck slot. It never changes the result of a Battle, the speed of progress or the content of the Collection.

We did this because the game director wants a rare currency that players can also buy, and [Pillar 1](../game/01-design-pillars.md) ("Fair to the player") must still be true. Money can buy how the game looks and how comfortable it is to use. Money can never buy power or faster progress.

## Considered Options

- **Cosmetics only.** Rejected. It is the safest option, but it excludes small comfort items, for example extra Deck slots, that do not give an advantage.
- **Heynstones also buy gameplay value (Packs, Gear, cards, Essence).** Rejected. This breaks Pillar 1 and the game vision. The 2023 revival of the original games has mostly negative reviews because of pay-to-win monetization.
- **A boost or a skip counts as a Convenience.** Rejected. XP or Coin boosts and Battle skips make progress faster. Thus a player with more money gets an advantage, and the Pillar 1 test fails.

## Consequences

- Coin, Heynstones and Essence never change into each other. If Heynstones could become Coin, money could buy power. If Coin could become Heynstones, players could farm Heynstones and they would not be rare.
- In v1, the total supply of Heynstones is fixed and smaller than the cost of the full Bazaar catalog. So the Player must choose what to unlock.
- Some Cosmetics are earn-only and never in the Bazaar. Players can get them only through play.
- Earned and bought Heynstones share one balance. Bundles for real money match the Bazaar price points.
- Each new Convenience must pass the Pillar 1 test before it goes into the Bazaar. After players pay for an item, it is very difficult to remove it.
- A Convenience can also cost Coin, because it gives no power.

## Update: a Deck slot costs Coin

An extra Deck slot was the v1 Convenience in the Bazaar, for 50 Heynstones. Now a new Player has 3 Deck slots and buys more with Coin, up to 10 ([Economy 3.6](../game/07-economy.md#36-deck-slots)). All players can get the slots through play. In v2, nobody pays real money to save more Decks. Thus in v1 the Bazaar sells only Cosmetics. The decision above does not change: Heynstones still buy only Cosmetics and Conveniences, and a later Convenience can still go into the Bazaar.
