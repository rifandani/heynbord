# Region Story Scenes: audio research

Access date for all online sources: **2026-10-27**.

Scope: audio assets, licenses, and optional English and Indonesian voice tools. Storyboards are outside this note. Voice is outside the trial. No audio was played, generated, or downloaded. No software was installed. No account was created. All sound choices below use creator descriptions and tags. Their use in Heynbord is a proposed fit, not a listening result.

## License terms and credit

- **CC0 1.0 Universal:** copying, modification, distribution, and commercial use are permitted without required credit. CC0 does not clear separate privacy, publicity, or trademark rights. See the [CC0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Kenney also confirms [commercial use and optional credit](https://kenney.nl/support).
- **CC BY 4.0:** commercial use and adaptation are permitted. Give appropriate credit, link the source and license, retain supplied notices, and identify changes. Do not suggest creator endorsement or add restrictions that remove the licensed freedoms. See the [CC BY 4.0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- Incompetech separates its [free credit-based option from its paid Standard License](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/licenses/). This shortlist uses the free CC BY option. Track descriptions and credit notices were available in indexed creator pages; direct page reads sometimes showed only a loading state. Check the current track notice again when selecting files.

For each MacLeod track used, record: **track title; Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com); source URL from the table; CC BY 4.0 with its license link; actual changes**. For example, list a trim, fade, or loop only if that change was made. The two track pages supply these titles, creator name, and license. No credit text has been added to the game.

## Optional voice routes: outside the trial

### ElevenLabs Multilingual v2: paid commercial route

Creator: ElevenLabs. Its [model documentation](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/models/) explicitly supports English and Indonesian for `eleven_multilingual_v2`. This can read custom scripts. Language support does not verify a suitable character voice or correct pronunciation of Heynbord names.

- **Source code:** this hosted route does not grant rights to the synthesis engine source. An SDK license would not license the hosted model or its output.
- **Model and voice:** proprietary service access under the [Terms of Service](https://elevenlabs.io/terms-of-use), including sections 4 and 5. Output rights do not include ownership of the model. Use a service voice or a voice that the team has rights to supply.
- **Output and price:** the [publication terms](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/legal/can-i-publish-the-content-i-generate-on-the-platform) permit commercial use of content generated during a paid subscription, including continued use after that subscription. Free-plan output is noncommercial and requires credit when published. Paying later does not clear earlier free output. Beta output is excluded from commercial use. Credit and other conditions depend on the agreement and [service-specific terms](https://elevenlabs.io/service-specific-terms). Output rights remain subject to these terms; they are not CC0 rights.

Open points: select a permitted voice, confirm its terms and non-Beta status, and check English and Indonesian delivery. No voice or paid plan was selected.

### Meta MMS: free download, excluded from commercial use

Creator: Meta. There are separate official checkpoints for [English, `mms-tts-eng`](https://huggingface.co/facebook/mms-tts-eng), and [Indonesian, `mms-tts-ind`](https://huggingface.co/facebook/mms-tts-ind). These can read custom text, but do not establish the same character voice across languages.

- **Source code:** the [MMS project license notice](https://github.com/facebookresearch/fairseq/blob/main/examples/mms/README.md#license) explicitly places MMS code and model weights under CC BY-NC 4.0. Do not substitute the parent repository's general license.
- **Model and voice:** both checkpoint cards specify CC BY-NC 4.0. The [license terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/) require credit, a license link, and change notices, and permit adaptation only for noncommercial use.
- **Output:** no separate grant that clears commercial game output was found in these sources. Free download does not establish that right. Exclude this route from commercial production unless separate permission is obtained.

### Piper: open-source engine, Indonesian voice terms unresolved

The current [Piper engine](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl) is GPL-3.0. Its [voice documentation](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl/blob/main/docs/VOICES.md) lists English and Indonesian and directs users to each voice's model card for its terms. The engine license does not establish the rights for every voice.

The Indonesian [`news_tts` model card](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/raw/main/id/id_ID/news_tts/medium/MODEL_CARD) identifies an Indonesian voice, but its dataset license entry only points to a Kaggle page titled "Indic TTS Malayalam Speech Corpus". The page did not provide readable license terms in this check. The model was also fine-tuned from the English `lessac` voice; that [model card](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/raw/main/en/en_US/lessac/medium/MODEL_CARD) points to separate dataset terms. These references do not verify commercial rights for the Indonesian voice or its generated story audio. Treat it as a research candidate, not a selected production voice. Voice quality was not checked.

No free, open-source voice route was verified here that meets all three needs: suitable story delivery, English plus Indonesian, and clear commercial rights for the code, voice/model, and output. The paid route is conditional. None of these routes changes the trial scope.
