# Glowcap Hollows Storyboard

Status: version 1, a draft. It continues the story of the [Red Mesas storyboard](./red-mesas-storyboard.md) and uses the same format: an opening, a Boss scene and an ending, with 5 Story Moments each. The text, lore and shots need the owner's review, the human listening review and the Player checks at the end of this page. The trial design is in [Region Story Scenes](../23-region-story-scenes.md).

Each moment is one 9-second fframes clip. The action finishes at 5 seconds. Seconds 5 to 9 are a seamless 4-second loop, so the picture stays alive while the Player reads. The Player controls reading time. Proper names keep their spelling in both Locales.

## Story spine

The Region tells one local adventure and opens one mystery.

- **Local adventure:** You come out of the deep tunnel into the Glowcap Hollows. Lady Glimmerbough, the grove warden, hired goblin diggers to wall her grove, and she pays them in glowcaps. You came out of a goblin tunnel, so the Elves think that you are one more digger. You climb to her Grove Hall and fight her. After the Battle, she sees that you are not a digger, and she sends Lethiel, First Gardener, with you.
- **Mystery:** Glimmerbough is not the buyer of Snikkit's painted gold. She is one more pawn. A "friend of the grove" writes to her up the deep tunnel. The friend warned her that diggers will take her grove, and the friend buys her glowcaps with amber. Those glowcaps paid Snikkit, and Snikkit's gold paid the Baron. The friend's letters have the seal of three lanes cut through. Each drop of the amber holds a small black thorn, and amber with black thorns comes only from the old trees of the Thornwood.

The pattern repeats on purpose. In each Region, the enemy of the Accord pays with something that comes from the next Region: painted red stone in Hearthvale, glowcaps in the Red Mesas, and Thornwood amber in the Glowcap Hollows. The Red Mesas made the Player suspect Lady Glimmerbough. The Boss scene turns that suspicion around: she is a victim of the same trick as Grukka. The clue does not name the friend, and the seal owner stays unknown.

## Shared direction

- Address the Hero as "you" and "kamu". The Hero stays outside the frame.
- Keep the narration and dialogue outside the video, so both Locales use the same clips. The only words in the video are the proper names GLOWCAP HOLLOWS and GLIMMERBOUGH.
- Use the existing paintings and card art: the Glowcap Hollows Region Map and Battle Painting, the Canopy Vinewarden, Bramble Nest and Lethiel card art, and a glimpse of the Thornwood Region Map. Graded light, glow, spores, roots, amber and the other props are made in code.
- Lady Glimmerbough has no card and no painting. The Region Map shows her small, at the steps of her hall, with a glowcap in her hand: Opening 3 pushes in on that figure. In the Boss scene she is a tall figure drawn in code, backlit by her hall, with a glowcap staff and two points of cyan light for eyes. A painted portrait can replace the drawn figure later.
- The palette is the Region's palette: moss green, deep violet and blue shade, the cyan and green glow of the mushrooms and warm brown roots. Two accents carry the mystery: the violet of the cut seal and, new in this Region, the warm amber of the Thornwood.
- The signature motion of the Region is light that wakes in a chain: mushroom after mushroom, step after step, line after line. Each moment uses it differently, and no moment reuses the camera move of the moment before it.
- With reduced motion, show the final poster of each moment and the same text.

## Opening

### 1. Out of the dark

**English:** The deep tunnel ends in light. You step out into the Glowcap Hollows, an old Elf grove where giant mushrooms shine.

**Indonesian:** Terowongan dalam itu berakhir dengan cahaya. Kamu melangkah keluar ke Glowcap Hollows, hutan tua para Elf tempat jamur-jamur raksasa bersinar.

**Shot:** The clip starts where the Red Mesas ending stops: the deep tunnel, the round glimpse of the Glowcap Hollows and the faint cut seal. The seal breaks into spores that the light pulls forward. The camera rushes through the glimpse, which opens to fill the frame in a cyan flash. Over the Region Map, a ring of light runs out from the tunnel exit and wakes the glowcaps one by one. Spores fly together into the title GLOWCAP HOLLOWS, which blooms with light.

**Loop:** Spores rise, fireflies drift, the mushrooms breathe light, a glint crosses the title.

**Sound:** A rail rumble that stops, a whoosh into the light, a rising chime for each wave of glowcaps.

### 2. The grove at dusk

**English:** At dusk, the glowcaps wake and the whole grove shines. The Elves keep it. Their wounds close, their arrows carry poison, and their roots hold fast.

**Indonesian:** Saat senja, glowcap terbangun dan seluruh hutan bersinar. Para Elf menjaganya. Luka mereka menutup sendiri, panah mereka membawa racun, dan akar mereka mengikat kuat.

**Shot:** The camera drifts low across the middle of the map, from the waterfalls toward the rope bridge. Dusk falls over the painting. The giant mushrooms light up in a chain, cap after cap, from left to right, and the waterfalls start to glow. Fireflies rise out of the shade. Three signs of the Elves follow the light: a leaf of green light that closes like a wound, a drop of poison that falls from a thorn, and a root that curls up out of the moss.

**Loop:** The caps breathe light, the falls shimmer, fireflies rise, the root sways.

**Sound:** Soft chimes as the caps light, a creak of the root.

### 3. Walls and traps

**English:** But the grove has new walls and traps. Lady Glimmerbough, the grove warden, hired goblin diggers to build them, and she pays them in glowcaps.

**Indonesian:** Tetapi hutan ini punya tembok dan jebakan baru. Lady Glimmerbough, penjaga hutan, menyewa para penggali goblin untuk membangunnya, dan ia membayar mereka dengan glowcap.

**Shot:** The camera pushes in on the steps of the Grove Hall, where the painted Lady Glimmerbough hands a glowcap to a goblin. The glowcap in her hand flares cyan. Then, from left to right, spiked walls of logs, roots and goblin scrap slam up out of the ground in front of the camera, each with a burst of moss and dirt. At the end a jaw trap snaps shut in the front.

**Loop:** The glowcap pulses, spores drift between the stakes, a goblin lamp on a wall swings.

**Sound:** A shimmer for the glowcap, three wooden slams, a metal snap.

### 4. An arrow

**English:** You came out of a goblin tunnel, so the Elves think that you are one more digger. They greet you with arrows.

**Indonesian:** Kamu keluar dari terowongan goblin, jadi para Elf mengira kamu penggali yang lain. Mereka menyambutmu dengan panah.

**Shot:** The view from the mouth of the goblin tunnel, framed by its timbers, at dusk. Pairs of eyes open in the shade under the mushrooms, cyan, one after another. A bow creaks. An arrow flies out of the dark straight at the camera and hits the timber next to it at 2.3 s. The arrow shakes, splinters fly and a green drop of poison runs down its point. More arrowheads glint in the shade.

**Loop:** The eyes blink, the arrowheads glint, spores and fireflies drift.

**Sound:** A bow creak, a whoosh, a hard wooden hit.

### 5. Your road

**English:** Climb to Glimmerbough's Grove Hall. The glowcaps that paid Snikkit grew in her grove, and you must learn who buys them.

**Indonesian:** Dakilah sampai ke Grove Hall milik Glimmerbough. Glowcap yang membayar Snikkit tumbuh di hutannya, dan kamu harus mencari tahu siapa pembelinya.

**Shot:** The map at dusk. A wisp of cyan light comes out of the tunnel and flies along the Trail. It leaves a ribbon of light, and each of the ten clearings blooms as it passes. The camera cranes up with it, over the shelf-fungus steps, to the Grove Hall. The windows of the hall flare.

**Loop:** The ribbon flows, the clearings breathe, the wisp circles the hall.

**Sound:** A rising chime for each clearing, a soft swell at the hall.

## Boss

The Boss scene plays when the Player selects Fight on Stage 3-10 for the first time. It is a conversation: Glimmerbough talks, and you answer. Each Story Moment has one to three lines. Continue shows the next line; the clip changes only at the next moment. After the last line, Fight starts the Battle. Skip also starts the Battle.

Glimmerbough is proud, calm and certain. She calls the Hero "digger" and speaks of "my grove" and "my friend". Her motive is fear for her grove: a friend that she never met warned her about diggers from the tunnels. The joke of the Region is that she hired diggers to keep the diggers out. The Hero shows her the chain of payments, but she does not believe it before the Battle.

In the lines below, `Glimmerbough:`, `Lethiel:` and `You:` are speaker labels. The game shows them as name plates, with the Hero portrait for `You:`. A line with no label is narration.

### 1. The Grove Hall

**English:** At the top of the shelf-fungus steps stands Glimmerbough's Grove Hall. Its door of woven roots opens by itself. / Glimmerbough: Another digger from the tunnels. You are brave, or very foolish.

**Indonesian:** Di puncak tangga jamur rak berdiri Grove Hall milik Glimmerbough. Pintunya yang dianyam dari akar terbuka dengan sendirinya. / Glimmerbough: Satu lagi penggali dari terowongan. Kamu berani, atau sangat bodoh.

**Shot:** Dusk on the map. The camera climbs the shelf-fungus steps; each step lights under it. At the top, the roots woven over the door pull apart, strand by strand, and warm light pours out down the steps. The windows of the hall light, and the mushroom roof starts to glow.

**Loop:** The door light breathes, spores rise, the roof glows, fireflies circle.

**Sound:** Soft chimes on the steps, a long creak of roots, a swell of light.

### 2. Glimmerbough

**English:** You: I am not a digger. I followed the glowcaps here from the Red Mesas. / Glimmerbough: Every digger says that. A friend of the grove told me that you would come.

**Indonesian:** You: Aku bukan penggali. Aku mengikuti glowcap sampai ke sini dari Red Mesas. / Glimmerbough: Semua penggali berkata begitu. Seorang sahabat hutan sudah memberitahuku bahwa kamu akan datang.

**Shot:** Inside the hall, under a tall window of green light. A column of spores spins in the middle and becomes Lady Glimmerbough, backlit: long pale hair, a mantle of leaves, a staff with a glowcap at its top. The glowcap blooms, a ring of light runs across the floor, and her eyes open as two cyan points. Her name plate grows letter by letter, and a vine draws its underline.

**Loop:** Her hair and mantle sway, the staff glowcap breathes, spores circle her, the window light shifts.

**Sound:** A magic swell, a bright chime for the staff, a low hit for the name.

### 3. The warning

**English:** Glimmerbough: My friend wrote to me. The Accord is failing, and diggers will come out of the tunnels to take my grove. / Glimmerbough: So I hired goblins to build my walls and set my traps. Goblins dig fast, and they work for mushrooms. / You: You hired diggers to keep the diggers out?

**Indonesian:** Glimmerbough: Sahabatku menulis surat kepadaku. Accord sedang runtuh, dan para penggali akan keluar dari terowongan untuk merebut hutanku. / Glimmerbough: Jadi aku menyewa goblin untuk membangun tembok dan memasang jebakan. Goblin menggali dengan cepat, dan mereka mau dibayar jamur. / You: Kamu menyewa penggali untuk menghalangi penggali?

**Shot:** A vision in Elf light, drawn with glowing lines on deep violet. A folded letter flies up out of a tunnel mouth and opens. Then a ring of mushrooms appears, and a wall with stakes draws itself around it. A goblin with a shovel runs along the wall, and a jaw trap snaps shut at its foot.

**Loop:** Light flows along the lines, the goblin digs, spores drift, the letter glows.

**Sound:** A paper sound, a chime for each line, a snap.

### 4. The trade

**English:** You: Who buys your glowcaps? / Glimmerbough: My friend. I send glowcaps down the deep tunnel, and amber comes back up. Good amber, like drops of sunlight. / You: Your glowcaps paid for Snikkit's painted gold, and that gold paid the Baron's sellswords. Your friend is using you.

**Indonesian:** You: Siapa yang membeli glowcap-mu? / Glimmerbough: Sahabatku. Aku mengirim glowcap ke bawah lewat terowongan dalam, lalu amber datang kembali ke atas. Amber yang bagus, seperti tetesan cahaya matahari. / You: Glowcap-mu membayar emas palsu Snikkit, dan emas itu membayar tentara bayaran sang Baron. Sahabatmu memanfaatkanmu.

**Shot:** A deep shaft under the grove, with roots on its walls. A basket full of glowcaps goes down on a vine rope, and its cyan light gets small in the dark. Far below, a warm light answers. The basket comes back up full of amber drops that shine like small suns. Drops spill over its rim and fall away into the dark in slow motion.

**Loop:** Amber drops fall and glint, the basket sways, cyan spores and warm motes drift.

**Sound:** A creak of the rope down, a deep hum, a warm shimmer as the amber rises.

### 5. The challenge

**English:** Glimmerbough: Enough lies! Roots, hold this digger. Vinewarden, guard the middle lane! / You: Then we fight. After this battle, read your friend's letters again.

**Indonesian:** Glimmerbough: Cukup bohongnya! Akar, ikat penggali ini. Vinewarden, jaga jalur tengah! / You: Kalau begitu kita bertarung. Setelah pertempuran ini, baca lagi surat-surat sahabatmu.

**Shot:** The mossy floor of the Battle Painting at dusk. Three lanes of cyan light race across. Roots burst out of the moss along the lanes. A Bramble Nest card slams into the middle Lane, and the Canopy Vinewarden card lands behind it with a ring of leaves and vines. Glimmerbough rises at the right, and her staff flares. This is the real start of the Boss Battle.

**Loop:** Energy flows along the lanes, the roots sway, the cards glow, spores rise.

**Sound:** A deep drum, root creaks, two card slams, a magic swell.

## Ending

### 1. The roots let go

**English:** The roots let go, and the grove goes quiet. Lady Glimmerbough lowers her staff and looks at you for a long time.

**Indonesian:** Akar-akar itu melepaskan cengkeramannya, dan hutan menjadi sunyi. Lady Glimmerbough menurunkan tongkatnya dan menatapmu lama sekali.

**Shot:** The Battle Painting in warm late light after the Battle. Thick roots cross the frame and hold it. They loosen, uncurl and sink back into the moss one by one. Leaves fall. The glowcaps around the floor light again, softly, and warm sun rays come back.

**Loop:** Leaves fall, sun rays move, motes drift, the glowcaps breathe.

**Sound:** A long root creak, leaves, a soft chime.

### 2. Two seals

**English:** You show her the cracked coin and the letter from Snikkit's vault. Her friend's letters have the same seal: three lanes, cut through.

**Indonesian:** Kamu menunjukkan koin yang retak dan surat dari ruang harta Snikkit. Surat-surat sahabatnya memiliki segel yang sama: tiga jalur yang terpotong.

**Shot:** A table of root wood, lit by a small glowcap. The cracked coin of painted stone drops onto it and rocks. Snikkit's letter slides in from the left. Then a second letter slides in from the right, and a third. Their violet seals light one after another, and a pulse of violet runs between them. They are the same.

**Loop:** The seals glow in turn, violet smoke rises, the glowcap breathes.

**Sound:** A coin, two paper slides, a low hit when the seals match.

### 3. The amber

**English:** She opens a box of her friend's amber. In every drop sleeps a small black thorn.

**Indonesian:** Ia membuka sekotak amber dari sahabatnya. Di dalam setiap tetesnya tertidur sebatang duri hitam kecil.

**Shot:** A carved box opens and warm light pours out. The camera pushes in on one amber drop. Light moves inside it like slow honey, and in its center a small black thorn turns into view.

**Loop:** Light flows in the amber, small bubbles rise, the thorn turns a little.

**Sound:** A wooden lid, a warm shimmer, a low tone for the thorn.

### 4. The First Gardener

**English:** Glimmerbough ends the goblins' work and opens her walls. Then she calls her First Gardener. / Glimmerbough: Lethiel, go with this hero. Find my friend, and bring me a name. / Lethiel: I know where black thorns grow. Follow me.

**Indonesian:** Glimmerbough menghentikan pekerjaan para goblin dan membuka temboknya. Lalu ia memanggil Tukang Kebun Pertamanya. / Glimmerbough: Lethiel, pergilah bersama pahlawan ini. Temukan sahabatku, dan bawakan aku sebuah nama. / Lethiel: Aku tahu di mana duri hitam tumbuh. Ikuti aku.

**Shot:** A spiral of seeds and petals blows in, and Lethiel's card art grows out of it. Vines draw the Epic card frame around Lethiel, with a green and gold glow, and flowers open along the frame.

**Loop:** Petals and seeds drift, the frame glows, the flowers sway.

**Sound:** A wind of leaves, a bright shimmer, a soft chime.

### 5. The Thornwood

**English:** Amber with black thorns comes from one place only: the old trees of the Thornwood. The trail goes on.

**Indonesian:** Amber berduri hitam hanya berasal dari satu tempat: pohon-pohon tua di Thornwood. Jejaknya berlanjut.

**Shot:** One amber drop fills the frame. The camera flies into it, and through the warm amber the Thornwood Region Map comes into view, in golden light. The black thorn floats over the forest, and the cut seal burns faintly in the amber.

**Loop:** Light flows in the amber, bubbles rise, the seal breathes, leaves drift.

**Sound:** A deep whoosh, a warm swell, the unanswered phrase of the theme.

## Player checks

- Can the Player say why the Hero is in the Glowcap Hollows after the opening?
- Can the Player say why Glimmerbough built walls, and who buys her glowcaps?
- Can the Player say why they would enter the Thornwood after the ending?
- Does the Player still feel sympathy for Glimmerbough after the Battle?
- Does the seal still make the Player curious, without a claim about who the enemy is?
- Do the moments feel worth watching, and is Skip easy to find?
