# Red Mesas Storyboard

Status: version 1, a draft. It continues the story of the [Hearthvale storyboard](./hearthvale-storyboard.md) and uses the same format: an opening, a Boss scene and an ending, with 5 Story Moments each. The text, lore and shots need the owner's review, the human listening review and the Player checks at the end of this page. The trial design is in [Region Story Scenes](./region-story-scenes.md).

Each moment is one 9-second fframes clip. The action finishes at 5 seconds. Seconds 5 to 9 are a seamless 4-second loop, so the picture stays alive while the Player reads. The Player controls reading time. Proper names keep their spelling in both Locales.

## Story spine

The Region tells one local adventure and opens one mystery.

- **Local adventure:** You follow the painted gold from Hearthvale to its source. Boss Snikkit paints red stones gold and pays the Orc tribes with them. You go up the rails to his Throne Hall and stop the trick. Warchief Grukka learns the truth and fights with you after that.
- **Mystery:** Snikkit sells his painted gold by the cartload to a buyer with no name and no face. The buyer pays in glowcaps, and every order comes up the deep tunnel under the seal of three lanes cut through. Glowcaps grow in only one place: the Glowcap Hollows.

The pattern repeats on purpose. In each Region, the enemy of the Accord pays with something that comes from the next Region. The Player can learn the pattern and guess where the trail goes. The clue does not name the buyer, and it does not say that Lady Glimmerbough is the buyer. The seal owner stays unknown.

## Shared direction

- Address the Hero as "you" and "kamu". The Hero stays outside the frame.
- Keep the narration and dialogue outside the video, so both Locales use the same clips. The only words in the video are the proper names RED MESAS and SNIKKIT.
- Use the existing paintings and card art: the Red Mesas Region Map and Battle Painting, the Dungeons cave painting, the Boss Snikkit, Warchief Grukka, Junk Barricade and Orc card art, and a glimpse of the Glowcap Hollows Region Map. Snikkit and Grukka are cut out of their card art. Graded light, dust, fire, particles and drawn props are made in code.
- The palette is the Region's palette: rust red, ochre, dusty orange and clear sky blue. Two new accents carry the mystery: the cyan of the glowcaps and the violet of the cut seal.
- One focal movement at a time. Each moment has its own motion idea; no moment reuses the camera move of the moment before it.
- With reduced motion, show the final poster of each moment and the same text.

## Opening

### 1. The red land

**English:** The trail of painted gold leads you east, to the Red Mesas. Here the Orc tribes live on top of the mesas, and they love a good fight.

**Indonesian:** Jejak emas palsu membawamu ke timur, ke Red Mesas. Di sini suku-suku Orc tinggal di puncak mesa, dan mereka sangat suka bertarung.

**Shot:** The clip starts where the Hearthvale ending stops: the Red Mesas map at dusk, red dust and the faint cut seal in the sky. The seal burns away into embers. The sun comes up at the upper left and a wave of morning light sweeps over the mesas. The camera comes down over the canyon, vultures circle, and the title RED MESAS slams down with a ring of dust.

**Loop:** Heat haze, circling vultures, a dust devil, sun rays.

**Sound:** Wind, then a deep impact on the title.

### 2. Under the mesas

**English:** Under the mesas, the Goblins dig. They dig for scrap, they sell scrap, and very often, they blow things up.

**Indonesian:** Di bawah mesa, para Goblin menggali. Mereka menggali rongsokan, menjual rongsokan, dan sangat sering, mereka meledakkan sesuatu.

**Shot:** The camera goes down through the ground into a cut-away view of the rock. Tunnels, rails and goblin lanterns show in the red stone. Mine carts run along the rails. A lit fuse runs to a powder barrel, and at 3.3 s the barrel explodes: a flash, dust and stones, and a jet of dust out of the mesa top.

**Loop:** The carts run on their rails, the lanterns swing, dust falls from the roof.

**Sound:** Rail rumble, the fuse, one explosion.

### 3. Boss Snikkit

**English:** Boss Snikkit, the goblin mine king, rules the mines. He pays the Orcs in gold, so the Orcs fight for him.

**Indonesian:** Boss Snikkit, raja tambang goblin, menguasai tambang-tambang itu. Ia membayar para Orc dengan emas, jadi para Orc bertarung untuknya.

**Shot:** A rail draws itself across the frame. Snikkit races in on it in his mine cart, with sparks under the wheels, and brakes hard at 1.9 s. A fountain of gold coins flies out of the cart to three Orc cards, which flip and glow. His name plate stamps in.

**Loop:** Torch flicker, fuse sparks on his bombs, the coins glint, the cart rocks.

**Sound:** Rail rumble, a metal skid, coins.

### 4. Painted stone

**English:** But you know this gold. Under the paint is red stone from these mesas. The Orcs do not know it yet.

**Indonesian:** Tetapi kamu kenal emas ini. Di balik catnya ada batu merah dari mesa-mesa ini. Para Orc belum mengetahuinya.

**Shot:** A pile of shining coins. A brass lens moves across the pile. Inside the lens, every coin is a red stone with gold paint that runs off it. The lens stops on one coin at the center, and a drop of paint falls.

**Loop:** The lens drifts a little, the paint drips, motes in the light.

**Sound:** A soft magic swell, a paint drip.

### 5. Your road

**English:** Follow the rails up to Snikkit's Throne Hall. Somebody buys his painted gold, and you must learn who.

**Indonesian:** Ikuti rel sampai ke Throne Hall milik Snikkit. Seseorang membeli emas palsunya, dan kamu harus mencari tahu siapa.

**Shot:** The Red Mesas map at midday. A lit fuse runs along the Trail from the Dust Road. Each of the ten clearings pops with dust as the spark goes past, and the camera follows it up to the Throne Hall. The bucket crown on the hall flashes.

**Loop:** The clearings pulse, smoke rises from the hall, the spark at the hall glows.

**Sound:** The fuse, small pops, a bright hit at the hall.

## Boss

The Boss scene plays when the Player selects Fight on Stage 2-10 for the first time. It is a conversation: Snikkit talks, and you answer. Warchief Grukka says one line before the Battle. Each Story Moment has one to three lines. Continue shows the next line; the clip changes only at the next moment. After the last line, Fight starts the Battle. Skip also starts the Battle.

Snikkit is a salesman. He calls everybody "customer", talks about himself by name, and he is proud of his paint. His motive is profit: Orcs want gold, Goblins have rocks, and paint is cheaper than gold. He also has a buyer that he never met, who pays in glowcaps. In the ending, Grukka learns the truth, so the line "nobody tells them" becomes the joke.

In the lines below, `Snikkit:`, `Grukka:` and `You:` are speaker labels. The game shows them as name plates, with the Hero portrait for `You:`. A line with no label is narration.

### 1. The Throne Hall

**English:** At the top of the rails stands Snikkit's Throne Hall, cut into the largest mesa. Its scrap gate rattles open. / Snikkit: Customers! Come in, come in! Mind the bombs.

**Indonesian:** Di ujung rel berdiri Throne Hall milik Snikkit, dipahat di mesa terbesar. Gerbang rongsokannya terbuka sambil berderak. / Snikkit: Pelanggan! Masuk, masuk! Awas bomnya.

**Shot:** Dusk. The camera climbs the last ramp to the Throne Hall. The scrap gate shakes, and its plates lift one by one. Furnace light spills out, the lanterns light in a chain, and a goblin rocket goes up and bursts into gold sparks over the bucket crown.

**Loop:** Furnace glow breathes, chimney smoke, lantern flicker, small sparks.

**Sound:** Metal rattles, a gate clank, a rocket and its burst.

### 2. Snikkit

**English:** Snikkit: So you are the one who followed Snikkit's coins all the way from Hearthvale! They travel far, eh? / You: Your coins paid the Baron's sellswords, Snikkit. And they are painted stone. / Snikkit: Painted with the finest paint! Paint is not cheap, you know.

**Indonesian:** Snikkit: Jadi kamu yang mengikuti koin-koin Snikkit jauh-jauh dari Hearthvale! Koinnya jalan-jalan jauh, ya? / You: Koinmu membayar tentara bayaran sang Baron, Snikkit. Dan koin itu batu yang dicat. / Snikkit: Dicat dengan cat terbaik! Cat itu tidak murah, tahu.

**Shot:** Inside the mine hall, in lantern light. Snikkit's cart comes out of the dark at the back, rides the rails toward the camera with sparks, and skids to a stop at 1.9 s with dust. His torch flares and a crest of gold spikes draws itself behind him. His name plate hammers in, letter by letter.

**Loop:** Torch flicker, fuse sparks, embers, Snikkit breathes.

**Sound:** Rail rumble, a long skid, a hit when the cart stops, three hammer hits.

### 3. The trick

**English:** Snikkit: Orcs love gold. Goblins have rocks. So Snikkit paints the rocks, and everybody is happy! / You: Grukka's warriors fight and bleed for those rocks. Will they be happy when they learn the truth? / Snikkit: Then nobody tells them. Right, customer?

**Indonesian:** Snikkit: Orc suka emas. Goblin punya batu. Jadi Snikkit mengecat batunya, dan semua senang! / You: Prajurit Grukka bertarung dan terluka demi batu-batu itu. Apa mereka akan senang saat tahu yang sebenarnya? / Snikkit: Kalau begitu jangan ada yang memberi tahu mereka. Ya kan, pelanggan?

**Shot:** Snikkit's paint works. A steam whistle blows and the gears start. Red stones ride a belt into a bubbling vat of gold paint, splash, and come out as shining coins that jump onto a growing stack.

**Loop:** The belt keeps running, stones splash into the vat, coins jump onto the stack, steam puffs.

**Sound:** A steam whistle, gears, paint splashes, coins.

### 4. The buyer

**English:** You: Who buys a whole cart of painted gold? / Snikkit: A very good customer! A note comes up the deep tunnel, and Snikkit sends a cart of gold down. The cart comes back full of glowcaps. / Snikkit: Real glowcaps, that shine in the dark! No name, no face. Who cares? Glowcaps sell!

**Indonesian:** You: Siapa yang membeli emas palsu satu kereta penuh? / Snikkit: Pelanggan yang sangat baik! Sebuah pesan datang dari terowongan dalam, lalu Snikkit mengirim satu kereta emas ke bawah. Keretanya kembali penuh glowcap. / Snikkit: Glowcap asli, yang bersinar dalam gelap! Tanpa nama, tanpa wajah. Siapa peduli? Glowcap laku keras!

**Shot:** The deep tunnel, with rails that go down into the dark. A cart of painted gold rolls away and becomes small. A cyan glow grows far down the tunnel, and the cart comes back full of glowing mushrooms. Their light fills the tunnel walls, and spores float up.

**Loop:** Spores drift, the glow breathes, a faint violet light flickers deep in the tunnel.

**Sound:** A rumble that goes away and comes back, a soft magic swell.

### 5. The challenge

**English:** Snikkit: Enough talk! Grukka, show our customer the way out! / Grukka: Hrrr. Gold is gold. Grukka fights. / You: Then fight, Warchief. After this battle, take a good look at your gold.

**Indonesian:** Snikkit: Cukup bicaranya! Grukka, tunjukkan jalan keluar kepada pelanggan kita! / Grukka: Hrrr. Emas ya emas. Grukka bertarung. / You: Kalau begitu bertarunglah, Warchief. Setelah pertempuran ini, lihat baik-baik emasmu.

**Shot:** The clay floor of the Battle Painting under a red sky. Three lanes of fire light race across. Two Junk Barricade cards slam into Lanes 1 and 3, then Warchief Grukka lands in the middle Lane with a shock ring and dust. Snikkit rises at the right with a fire rim light. This is the real start of the Boss Battle.

**Loop:** Energy flows along the lanes, the cards glow, embers rise, Snikkit's torch flickers.

**Sound:** War drums, two metal slams, a heavy landing and a roar.

## Ending

### 1. The crown falls

**English:** Snikkit's carts stop. His great bucket crown falls off and rolls across the clay, until it rocks to a stop.

**Indonesian:** Kereta-kereta Snikkit berhenti. Mahkota embernya yang besar terjatuh dan menggelinding di atas tanah liat, sampai akhirnya bergoyang dan berhenti.

**Shot:** The clay floor of the Battle Painting, in clear midday light after the Battle. The bucket crown drops in from above, hits the clay with a ring of dust and bounces. It tips onto its side, rolls along a curve with its spikes turning, and rocks to a stop. Dust settles.

**Loop:** Dust motes in the sun, heat haze, a glint on the crown.

**Sound:** A metal clank, a rolling rattle, a last ring.

### 2. The bite

**English:** Grukka wants his pay. He bites one of Snikkit's coins, and it cracks. Under the gold is stone.

**Indonesian:** Grukka menuntut upahnya. Ia menggigit salah satu koin Snikkit, dan koin itu retak. Di balik emasnya ada batu.

**Shot:** Grukka, from the waist up, against the sunset. A coin flips up to his tusks. Crunch: the coin cracks, gold flakes fly and red stone falls. His eyes flare red, and the frame shakes.

**Loop:** A red rim light pulses, embers rise, flakes drift.

**Sound:** A crunch, falling stone, a low growl.

### 3. The Warchief

**English:** The Orcs fought for stones. Grukka's roar shakes the mesas. Then he puts on the bucket crown and turns to you. / Grukka: You fight fair. Grukka fights with you now.

**Indonesian:** Para Orc bertarung demi batu. Raungan Grukka mengguncang mesa. Lalu ia memakai mahkota ember itu dan berbalik kepadamu. / Grukka: Kamu bertarung dengan adil. Sekarang Grukka bertarung bersamamu.

**Shot:** Grukka roars, and rings of sound shake the dust. Orc banners rise behind him. The bucket crown drops onto his horned helmet with a clank and sits crooked. His Epic card frame draws itself around him with a gold and orange glow.

**Loop:** The banners wave, the card frame glows, embers rise.

**Sound:** A roar, war drums, a metal clank, a bright shimmer.

### 4. The payment

**English:** In Snikkit's vault, you find what the buyer paid: crates of glowcaps. Under them is a letter with a seal of three lanes, cut through.

**Indonesian:** Di ruang harta Snikkit, kamu menemukan bayaran dari si pembeli: peti-peti berisi glowcap. Di bawahnya ada sepucuk surat dengan segel tiga jalur yang terpotong.

**Shot:** A dark vault. The lid of a crate jumps off, and cyan light floods out of it. Glowcaps fill the crate and spores rise. A letter slides out from under the crate, and its wax seal glows violet: the three lanes of the Accord, cut by one slash.

**Loop:** Spores rise, the cyan glow breathes, violet smoke rises from the seal.

**Sound:** A crate lid, a soft magic swell, a low hit for the seal.

### 5. The deep tunnel

**English:** Glowcaps grow in only one place: the Glowcap Hollows. The deep tunnel leads there, and so does the trail.

**Indonesian:** Glowcap hanya tumbuh di satu tempat: Glowcap Hollows. Terowongan dalam itu menuju ke sana, begitu pula jejaknya.

**Shot:** The camera flies along the rails into the deep tunnel. Wooden frames rush past, and the cyan light ahead grows. At 3.3 s the tunnel opens on a glimpse of the Glowcap Hollows map, and the cut seal burns faintly in its glow.

**Loop:** Spores drift, the glow breathes, the faint seal breathes.

**Sound:** Rail rumble that builds, a whoosh into the light, the unanswered phrase of the theme.

## Player checks

- Can the Player say why the Hero is in the Red Mesas after the opening?
- Can the Player say what Snikkit does with the stones, and who pays him?
- Can the Player say why they would enter the Glowcap Hollows after the ending?
- Does the seal still make the Player curious, without a claim about who the enemy is?
- Do the moments feel worth watching, and is Skip easy to find?
