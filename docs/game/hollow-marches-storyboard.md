# Hollow Marches Storyboard

Status: version 1, a draft. It continues the story of the [Thornwood storyboard](./thornwood-storyboard.md) and uses the same format: an opening, a Boss scene and an ending, with 5 Story Moments each. The text, lore and shots need the owner's review, the human listening review and the Player checks at the end of this page. The trial design is in [Region Story Scenes](./region-story-scenes.md).

Each moment is one 9-second fframes clip. The action finishes at 5 seconds. Seconds 5 to 9 are a seamless 4-second loop, so the picture stays alive while the Player reads. The Player controls reading time. Proper names keep their spelling in both Locales.

## Story spine

The Region tells one local adventure, closes the misunderstanding and opens the last door of the Campaign.

- **Local adventure:** The coins of bone lead you and the Mountain Colossus out of the Thornwood into the Hollow Marches. The marsh never freezes, but now frost covers the reeds. When the Old Bramble woke, the noise woke the frost beasts in the ice caves of the hills, and they came down into the marsh on their own. The dead of the marsh fled from them. Then you walk in right behind the beasts, with a stone giant. The court of Queen Marrow sees the Colossus and decides that you sent the beasts and came to take the castle. The Bone Ferry turns you away, and chains close the Witchfire Bridge. You fight to Marrow Castle. After the Battle, the court knows that you did not send the beasts, and the Queen gives you her respect and a Bone Rampart from her castle wall.
- **Mystery:** Queen Marrow pays her dead in coins of bone, so the Player can suspect her. The Boss scene clears her: someone spent her coin without her leave, and she did not pay the cutters. The real clue is in the purse. One coin is not bone. It is pale tower stone with a small tower cut into it, and the Queen knows the stone: Heynspire, the white tower above your own Town. The cut seal on the purse burns violet, and nobody explains it.

The pattern repeats one last time. In each Region, the enemy of the Accord pays with something that comes from the next place: painted red stone in Hearthvale, glowcaps in the Red Mesas, Thornwood amber in the Glowcap Hollows, coins of bone in the Thornwood, and a coin of tower stone in the Hollow Marches. The Thornwood made the Player suspect Queen Marrow, as Queen Marrow suspects you. The Boss scene turns both suspicions around in one exchange. The clue points beyond the five Regions, to the tower that the Player sees in the Town every day. It does not name the enemy, and the seal owner stays unknown.

## Shared direction

- Address the Hero as "you" and "kamu". The Hero stays outside the frame.
- Keep the narration and dialogue outside the video, so both Locales use the same clips. The only words in the video are the proper names HOLLOW MARCHES, QUEEN MARROW and HEYNSPIRE.
- Use the existing paintings and card art: the Hollow Marches Region Map and Battle Painting, the Undead card art (Lantern Widow, Hushbow, Coffin Lancer, Chattering Cohort, Grave Bell Tender, Graveyard Drudge, Rattleknife and Bone Rampart), the cold Feral card art (Woolly Mammoth, Avalanche Yeti, Frost Elk Matriarch, Frostfang Lynx and Old Frostmaw), the Mountain Colossus card art as a silhouette and the Blizzard card art. The court and the beasts are cut out of their card art and re-lit. Frost, ice, mist, the hall, the ferry, the chains, the coins, the purse and Heynspire are made in code.
- Queen Marrow has no card and no portrait. She is drawn in code: tall and thin, a crown of bone, violet robes, a calm mask of bone and soft green light in the eye sockets. She is elegant, not scary. A painted portrait can replace her later.
- The Mountain Colossus is a presence: a shadow on the water, a silhouette behind a window, a shape that kneels. It never fights on screen.
- The palette is the Region's palette: misty teal, violet, moss green, ice blue, bone white and soft green glow, with the warm key light from the upper left. Each moment has one accent: witchfire green, ice blue, violet seal, bone white or dawn gold.
- The signature motion of the Region is the mist tide: a slow wave of mist that rises and falls back. It appears in every moment at a different scale, from a breath on the water to a spiral in the blizzard.
- No two moments in a row use the same camera move. Each Story Scene has one shot that feels impossible: the camera rises up through the ice (Opening 2), flies through the ribs of the hall (Boss 1) and passes through a frosted window as the frost melts (Ending 1). No earlier Region uses these tricks.
- With reduced motion, show the final poster of each moment and the same text.

## Opening

### 1. The marsh that should not freeze

**English:** The coins of bone lead you and the Mountain Colossus into the Hollow Marches. Frost covers the reeds, but this marsh never freezes.

**Indonesian:** Koin-koin tulang menuntunmu dan Mountain Colossus masuk ke Hollow Marches. Embun beku menutupi alang-alang, padahal rawa ini tidak pernah membeku.

**Shot:** The clip starts where the Thornwood ending stops: the Hollow Marches map in cold mist, with the ferry lamp, the green bowls of the Witchfire Bridge and the faint cut seal. The seal sinks into the mist. The camera pulls back over the whole marsh while a wave of mist rolls across the water from the left. Reeds rise into the foreground corners, and frost runs up their stems where no frost should be. The sun breaks through from the upper left, and the title HOLLOW MARCHES grows out of frost crystals.

**Loop:** The mist tide rises and falls, snow drifts, frost glitters on the reeds and the title, the reeds sway.


**Sound:** A cold wind, a deep whoosh as the mist rolls, the crackle of growing frost, a soft impact on the title.

### 2. The frost beasts

**English:** When the Old Bramble woke, the noise woke the frost beasts in the ice caves. They came down into the marsh on their own, and the dead of the marsh fled.

**Indonesian:** Saat Old Bramble terbangun, suaranya membangunkan binatang-binatang es di gua-gua es. Mereka turun ke rawa atas kemauan sendiri, dan para mayat hidup penghuni rawa melarikan diri.

**Shot:** The impossible shot of the opening. The camera is under the ice of a frozen bog pool. Light comes down through the ice from the upper left, and the dark shapes of great beasts cross over the ice in a line. Below, small skeletons of the marsh run away along the bottom, with bubbles. The camera rises up through the ice: the ice cracks and its shards fly past. Above, the frost beasts walk out of the Ice Cave and down the hill: a Woolly Mammoth, an Avalanche Yeti, a Frost Elk Matriarch, a Frostfang Lynx and Old Frostmaw. A tremor comes from the far west, with a golden flash and a ring in the mist: the Old Bramble woke. Snow falls from the trees.

**Loop:** The beasts walk out of the cave, their breath rolls down the hill as a low tide of mist, snow falls, frost glitters on the rocks.


**Sound:** Deep underwater ice groans, the ice cracks, heavy steps, a distant boom and rumble.

### 3. The stone giant

**English:** Then you walk in right behind them with a stone giant. The court of Queen Marrow sees the Colossus, and a wisp flies to Marrow Castle with the alarm.

**Indonesian:** Lalu kamu datang tepat di belakang mereka bersama sebuah raksasa batu. Para punggawa Queen Marrow melihat Colossus itu, dan sebuah wisp terbang ke Marrow Castle membawa tanda bahaya.

**Shot:** The map in late afternoon light. A huge shadow of the Mountain Colossus slides over the water and the Long Boardwalk; each step pushes out a ring of water and a breath of mist. A stone foot steps through the foreground, out of focus. In the reeds, a wisp of ice-blue light wakes and flies away over the marsh with a long trail. The camera follows it to Marrow Castle. The windows of the castle flare green one by one, and the violet banners snap in the wind.

**Loop:** The shadow breathes on the water, rings spread, the windows glow, the banners wave, frost dust drifts.


**Sound:** Two heavy steps with splashes, a whoosh for the wisp, a grave bell from the castle.

### 4. The crossings close

**English:** The court decides that you sent the beasts and came to take the castle. The Bone Ferry turns you away, and chains close the Witchfire Bridge.

**Indonesian:** Para punggawa memutuskan bahwa kamulah yang mengirim binatang-binatang itu dan datang untuk merebut kastel. Bone Ferry menolakmu, dan rantai-rantai menutup Witchfire Bridge.

**Shot:** The Bone Ferry close in the foreground, sharp: a long black boat with a bone prow and a lantern, and its skeleton ferryman in a hood. The ferryman raises an open hand of bone, and the boat turns away from you. The focus moves to the Witchfire Bridge behind it. The green fire bowls flare one after another, and heavy chains rise out of the water across the bridge, dripping. A curtain of mist rises behind the chains.

**Loop:** The green fires flicker, the chains sway and drip, the ferry rocks on the water, green motes rise.


**Sound:** A splash, two flares of fire, a long chain rattle, a heavy clank.

### 5. The road to Marrow Castle

**English:** Fight your way to Marrow Castle. Only Queen Marrow pays in coins of bone, so the answer is behind her gate.

**Indonesian:** Bertarunglah sampai ke Marrow Castle. Hanya Queen Marrow yang membayar dengan koin tulang, jadi jawabannya ada di balik gerbangnya.

**Shot:** The map. A line of cold green fire runs along the Trail from the old stone road, and each of the ten clearings lights with a ring of green flames as it passes. The camera tracks with the fire, over the boardwalks and the causeway. The mist pulls back from the road in front of it. At the end, the gate of Marrow Castle opens, light spills down the steps, and a tall, thin shadow stands in it.

**Loop:** The green rings breathe, light flows along the road, the gate glows, the banners wave.


**Sound:** A soft flare for each clearing, a gate that opens, a low bell.

## Boss

The Boss scene plays when the Player selects Fight on Stage 5-10 for the first time. It is a conversation: Queen Marrow talks, and you answer. Each Story Moment has one to three lines. Continue shows the next line; the clip changes only at the next moment. After the last line, Fight starts the Battle. Skip also starts the Battle.

Queen Marrow is very old, very calm and very dry. She speaks in short, exact sentences and never shouts. She is proud of her marsh and her dead. She is not a villain: her marsh is frozen, her dead are fleeing, and a stranger arrives with a stone giant. The joke of the Region is that your best clue, the purse of her coins, makes you look like a thief of her dead's wages. The reversal is that she enjoys the insult: someone spent her coin without her leave, and nobody has been so rude to her in 300 years. She fights you because she has missed a good fight.

In the lines below, `Queen Marrow:` and `You:` are speaker labels. The game shows them as name plates, with the Hero portrait for `You:`. A line with no label is narration.

### 1. The bored queen

**English:** Queen Marrow has ruled the Hollow Marches for 300 quiet years. She does not stand up for guests. / Queen Marrow: Do come in. Your giant can wait outside; it is dripping on my moat.

**Indonesian:** Queen Marrow sudah memerintah Hollow Marches selama 300 tahun yang tenang. Ia tidak pernah berdiri untuk menyambut tamu. / Queen Marrow: Silakan masuk. Raksasamu tunggu di luar saja; ia meneteskan air ke paritku.

**Shot:** The impossible shot of the Boss scene. The camera flies through the hall of Marrow Castle, through arch after arch of great white ribs. Mist pours along the floor between them. At the end of the hall sits Queen Marrow on a throne of bone, under a tall window. Her chin rests on one hand, and the fingers of the other hand tap the arm of the throne. Outside the window, the dark shape of the Colossus waits in the mist. The name plate QUEEN MARROW forms in bone-white letters under a violet glow.

**Loop:** The mist flows along the floor, her fingers tap, the eye lights glow, motes drift in the window light.


**Sound:** A wind through the ribs, a low bell, slow taps of bone, a soft impact for the name.

### 2. The accusation

**English:** Queen Marrow: My dead are fleeing. My marsh is frozen. / Queen Marrow: And then you arrive, with a stone giant. / You: I did not send the beasts. I followed a trail here.

**Indonesian:** Queen Marrow: Rakyat matiku melarikan diri. Rawaku membeku. / Queen Marrow: Lalu kamu datang, bersama raksasa batu. / You: Bukan aku yang mengirim binatang-binatang itu. Aku datang ke sini mengikuti jejak.

**Shot:** Closer on the Queen. She leans forward on her throne, and the green light in her eyes flares. The camera arcs around her. Behind her, a curtain of mist slides across the tall window, hides the Colossus and then shows it again.

**Loop:** The mist curtain moves, frost dust drifts, her eyes glow, her robe stirs.


**Sound:** A low drone of the window wind, a cold flare as her eyes light, a soft rattle of bone.

### 3. The proof that makes it worse

**English:** You: The trail is this purse, full of your coins of bone. / Queen Marrow: Oh, that is good. You carry my dead's wages. / Queen Marrow: Thieves are usually shyer.

**Indonesian:** You: Jejaknya adalah kantong ini, penuh koin tulangmu. / Queen Marrow: Oh, bagus sekali. Kamu membawa upah rakyat matiku. / Queen Marrow: Biasanya pencuri lebih pemalu.

**Shot:** Coins of bone fall onto the white stone floor. They bounce, spin and roll, and a ring of cold mist puffs out where they land. The camera pulls back. The court stands in a row behind the coins: a Lantern Widow, a Hushbow, a Coffin Lancer and a Chattering Cohort. They step back from the coins. Behind them, Queen Marrow laughs on her throne: her shoulders shake, and her eyes curve into green crescents.

**Loop:** One coin still wobbles, the lanterns of the court sway, the Queen's laugh settles into a smile, frost dust drifts.


**Sound:** Coins on stone, a rattle of bone, a soft gasp of wind from the court.

### 4. Someone spent her coin

**English:** You: These coins paid the cutters who woke the Old Bramble in the Thornwood. / Queen Marrow: Then someone spent my coin without my leave. / Queen Marrow: In 300 years, nobody has been so rude to me. How delightful.

**Indonesian:** You: Koin-koin ini membayar para penyayat yang membangunkan Old Bramble di Thornwood. / Queen Marrow: Kalau begitu, seseorang membelanjakan koinku tanpa izinku. / Queen Marrow: Dalam 300 tahun, belum pernah ada yang sekurang ajar ini padaku. Menyenangkan sekali.

**Shot:** One coin of bone between two long fingers of bone, close and sharp, with cold breath rising from it. The Queen's mask is out of focus behind it, still laughing. The focus moves from the coin to her face. The laugh stops, and her eye lights go round and still. Then they slowly grow bright, and her head tilts: she is delighted.

**Loop:** Cold breath rises from the coin, the fingers turn it a little, her eyes glow, frost dust drifts.


**Sound:** A coin ring, a cold silence, a soft rising shimmer.

### 5. The challenge

**English:** Queen Marrow: Find my thief later. First, you will entertain me. / Queen Marrow: Rampart, hold the middle lane. And now, a little snow. / You: Then we fight, Your Majesty. After that, we find your thief.

**Indonesian:** Queen Marrow: Cari pencuriku nanti. Sekarang, hiburlah aku. / Queen Marrow: Rampart, jaga jalur tengah. Dan sekarang, sedikit salju. / You: Kalau begitu kita bertarung, Yang Mulia. Setelah itu, kita cari pencurimu.

**Shot:** The grassy island of the Battle Painting. Three lanes of pale light race across. A wall of bones bursts out of the ground in the middle Lane, and the Bone Rampart card rises out of it. A Hushbow card drops into the same Lane behind it. Queen Marrow rises to her full height at the right: she stands for you. The Blizzard card glows in her raised hand, a blizzard spirals in around her, and the frame turns with it. This is the real start of the Boss Battle: a Bone Rampart in Lane 2, Column 10, with a Hushbow behind it in Column 11, and Blizzard in her hand.

**Loop:** The snow spirals, energy flows along the lanes, the Rampart glows, her robe and the banners stream in the wind.


**Sound:** War drums, a crash of bones, a card slam, a long blizzard wind.

## Ending

### 1. The thaw

**English:** The hall goes quiet. The Old Bramble sleeps again, so the frost beasts climb back to their ice caves, and the marsh thaws.

**Indonesian:** Aula menjadi sunyi. Old Bramble sudah tidur lagi, jadi binatang-binatang es kembali mendaki ke gua-gua es mereka, dan rawa mencair.

**Shot:** The impossible shot of the ending. Frost ferns cover the tall window of the hall, with dawn light behind them, and the Queen's faint reflection stands in the glass. The frost melts from the edges inward and runs down in drops. The camera passes through the glass. Outside, the marsh warms in dawn gold, and the frost beasts walk back up the hill into the Ice Cave. The mist tide goes back up the hills with them.

**Loop:** The last drops fall past the camera, the beasts walk home, mist drifts up the hill, gold motes rise.


**Sound:** Melting drips, a soft glass ring, a warm wind, distant heavy steps.

### 2. Loud

**English:** The court lowers its weapons. You did not send the beasts, and now they know it. / Queen Marrow: You are loud. I like loud.

**Indonesian:** Para punggawa menurunkan senjata mereka. Bukan kamu yang mengirim binatang-binatang itu, dan sekarang mereka tahu. / Queen Marrow: Kamu berisik. Aku suka yang berisik.

**Shot:** The hall in dawn light. The camera trucks along the court: one by one, the Lantern Widow, the Hushbow, the Coffin Lancer and the Chattering Cohort bow and lower their weapons, and the green fire in their eyes softens. The mist falls away from their feet. At the end of the line sits Queen Marrow, one hand raised in a small, dry wave.

**Loop:** The lanterns sway, the mist settles, dust drifts in the light, the Queen's eyes glow.


**Sound:** Weapons lowered on stone, a soft rattle of bone, a warm chime.

### 3. Tower stone

**English:** The Queen empties the cutters' purse. One coin is not bone: it is pale stone, with a small tower cut into it. / Queen Marrow: Tower stone. Heynspire. / Queen Marrow: Nobody should be able to take stone from that tower.

**Indonesian:** Sang Ratu menuang isi kantong para penyayat. Satu koin bukan dari tulang: koin itu dari batu pucat, dengan ukiran menara kecil. / Queen Marrow: Batu menara. Heynspire. / Queen Marrow: Seharusnya tidak ada yang bisa mengambil batu dari menara itu.

**Shot:** The white stone floor of the hall. Queen Marrow holds the cutters' purse upside down, and coins of bone pour out onto the floor in a cold mist. Frost covers them. One coin falls last and lands face up: pale grey-white stone, with a tiny tower carved on its face. Frost does not touch it. The cut seal on the purse burns violet. The stone coin rises on a thread of green light, and the camera tilts up with it to her raised fingers. Her eye lights narrow: she is cautious, not afraid.

**Loop:** The seal burns and breathes, mist rises from the bone coins, light glints on the tower mark, her eyes glow.


**Sound:** A purse, a rattle of coins, a stone coin ringing alone, a low hit for the seal.

### 4. Bone Rampart

**English:** Queen Marrow gives you a Bone Rampart from her own castle wall, and the Mountain Colossus kneels to her. / Queen Marrow: Take a piece of my wall. I have plenty, and you will need it.

**Indonesian:** Queen Marrow memberimu sebuah Bone Rampart dari dinding kastelnya sendiri, dan Mountain Colossus berlutut kepadanya. / Queen Marrow: Ambillah sepotong dindingku. Aku punya banyak, dan kamu akan membutuhkannya.

**Shot:** A section of the bone-white castle wall. Its blocks fold over one by one, like tiles, and each tile turns into a part of the Bone Rampart card art. The Epic frame draws itself around the card and closes with a flash. The camera arcs around the card. Behind it, the dark shape of the Mountain Colossus kneels in the mist, and dust rolls out from its knee.

**Loop:** The Epic frame glows, bone dust drifts, mist wraps the card, light runs along the frame.


**Sound:** Stone blocks turning, a heavy thud, a bright shimmer.

### 5. Heynspire

**English:** Queen Marrow points to the white tower on the far hill: Heynspire, above your own Town. The trail leads home.

**Indonesian:** Queen Marrow menunjuk menara putih di bukit jauh: Heynspire, di atas Kotamu sendiri. Jejaknya mengarah pulang.

**Shot:** The camera cranes up from Marrow Castle, over the marsh and the icy hills, into the dawn. The mist parts like a curtain. On the far hill stands a tall, thin white tower with blue roofs. It rises into the clouds, and its top is not visible. A line of dawn light runs along the road from the castle, and the road turns toward the tower. The title HEYNSPIRE appears in cold white light. This is the last image of the Campaign, and it holds as a poster.

**Loop:** The mist breathes around the hill, the tower windows glow, the floating stones drift, snow and gold motes drift in the dawn.


**Sound:** A cold wind, a deep whoosh as the mist parts, a single low bell, the unanswered phrase of the theme.

## Player checks

- Can the Player say why the Hero is in the Hollow Marches after the opening?
- Can the Player say why the court of Queen Marrow fights the Hero?
- Can the Player say what the victory changed?
- Can the Player say why the Hero goes to Heynspire after the ending?
- Can the Player say who paid the cutters? The correct answer is "nobody knows yet".
- Does the seal still make the Player curious, without a claim about who the enemy is?
- Does the Player repeat a line of Queen Marrow?
- Do the moments feel worth watching, and is Skip easy to find?
