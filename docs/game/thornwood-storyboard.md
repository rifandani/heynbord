# Thornwood Storyboard

Status: version 1, a draft. It continues the story of the [Glowcap Hollows storyboard](./glowcap-hollows-storyboard.md) and uses the same format: an opening, a Boss scene and an ending, with 5 Story Moments each. The text, lore and shots need the owner's review, the human listening review and the Player checks at the end of this page. The trial design is in [Region Story Scenes](./region-story-scenes.md).

Each moment is one 9-second fframes clip. The action finishes at 5 seconds. Seconds 5 to 9 are a seamless 4-second loop, so the picture stays alive while the Player reads. The Player controls reading time. Proper names keep their spelling in both Locales.

## Story spine

The Region tells one local adventure and opens one mystery.

- **Local adventure:** Lethiel leads you into the Thornwood. The Elves of the Thornwood sing the Old Bramble to sleep: a forest giant that sleeps on a hill of brambles. When it wakes, the Feral beasts in the caves under its roots wake with it. Someone cuts the giant at night and takes its amber, so the Elves trust no stranger. They see Lethiel with you, and they think that you took the First Gardener prisoner. They block the forest road and call the beasts. The noise of the Battles wakes the Old Bramble, and it wakes up angry. After the Battle, Lethiel closes its cuts, and it goes back to sleep. Its Mountain Colossus goes with you.
- **Mystery:** The amber with black thorns is the Old Bramble's own amber. Cutters came at night with lanterns of cold green fire and took it. Each cut woke the giant a little more, so the Hero suspects that the cutters wanted it awake. The cutters ran when it woke. Their empty camp holds their pay: coins of bone, cold as ice, in a purse with the seal of three lanes cut through. Coins of bone are paid only in the Hollow Marches, where a dead ferryman takes travelers over the water.

The pattern repeats on purpose. In each Region, the enemy of the Accord pays with something that comes from the next Region: painted red stone in Hearthvale, glowcaps in the Red Mesas, Thornwood amber in the Glowcap Hollows and coins of bone in the Thornwood. The Glowcap Hollows made the Player suspect the Thornwood. The Boss scene shows that the Old Bramble is a victim, not a buyer. The clue does not name the enemy, and the seal owner stays unknown. The coins point at the Hollow Marches, so the Player can suspect Queen Marrow, as Queen Marrow will suspect the Hero in Region 5.

## Shared direction

- Address the Hero as "you" and "kamu". The Hero stays outside the frame.
- Keep the narration and dialogue outside the video, so both Locales use the same clips. The only words in the video are the proper names THORNWOOD and THE OLD BRAMBLE.
- Use the existing paintings and card art: the Thornwood Region Map and Battle Painting, the Bristleback Boar, Bramble Nest and Mountain Colossus card art, and a glimpse of the Hollow Marches Region Map. Graded light, bark, amber, leaves, the cutters, the coins and the other props are made in code.
- The Old Bramble has no card. The Region Map shows its face in the bark of the hill. Boss 2, Boss 4 and Ending 1 show the face close up, drawn in code: brows of bark, eyes of amber light, a beard of moss and a crown of red leaves. A painted portrait can replace the drawn face later.
- The palette is the Region's palette: deep emerald, moss green, teal shade, gold light through the leaves and some autumn red. The accents are the warm amber of the giant, the violet of the cut seal and, new in this Region, the cold green of the cutters' fire and the bone white of the coins.
- The signature motion of the Region is the giant's breath: a slow wave that moves through the leaves, the light and the ground. Each moment uses it differently, and no moment reuses the camera move of the moment before it.
- With reduced motion, show the final poster of each moment and the same text.

## Opening

### 1. Into the Thornwood

**English:** Lethiel leads you into the Thornwood, a deep, old forest of giant trees. Here even the hedges have thorns as long as spears.

**Indonesian:** Lethiel menuntunmu masuk ke Thornwood, hutan tua yang dalam dengan pohon-pohon raksasa. Di sini, bahkan pagar semaknya punya duri sepanjang tombak.

**Shot:** The clip starts where the Glowcap Hollows ending stops: inside the amber, over the Thornwood map, with the black thorn and the faint cut seal. The amber melts and runs off the frame like honey, and the seal burns away into golden motes. The afternoon light comes through: sun rays fall from the upper left, and the camera glides down over the forest. Two brambles grow in from the sides, and their thorns and red leaves draw the title THORNWOOD. Birds burst out of the trees.

**Loop:** Sun rays move, leaves fall, birds circle, a glint crosses the title, the brambles sway.

**Sound:** A wind of leaves, a creak as the brambles grow, bird wings, a soft impact on the title.

### 2. The sleeping giant

**English:** On a hill of brambles sleeps the Old Bramble, a forest giant. The Elves sing it to sleep. When it wakes, the beasts in the caves under its roots wake with it.

**Indonesian:** Di atas bukit semak berduri tidur Old Bramble, sang raksasa hutan. Para Elf bernyanyi agar ia tetap tidur. Saat ia bangun, binatang-binatang buas di gua di bawah akarnya ikut bangun.

**Shot:** The camera cranes slowly up the map toward the Bramble Hill. The giant breathes: its crown of leaves rises and falls, and a wave of light runs through the forest with each breath. Ribbons of golden song rise from the Lantern Tree and the Treetop Village and wrap around the giant's head, and its half-open eye drifts nearly shut. In the dark mouth of the Web Cave under the roots, pairs of beast eyes open, glow with each breath of the giant, and blink.

**Loop:** The giant breathes, the song flows, the eyes in the cave blink, leaves drift.

**Sound:** A slow wooden groan for each breath, soft chimes for the song, a low growl from the cave.

### 3. The cuts

**English:** But at night, someone cuts the giant's bark and takes its amber: amber with black thorns. The Elves do not know who, so they trust no stranger.

**Indonesian:** Tetapi pada malam hari, seseorang menyayat kulit kayu sang raksasa dan mengambil ambernya: amber berduri hitam. Para Elf tidak tahu siapa pelakunya, jadi mereka tidak percaya pada orang asing.

**Shot:** A close view of the giant's bark in blue moonlight. A cold green light comes in from the left, from a lantern outside the frame. A blade glints, and three cuts open in the bark one after another, with splinters. Amber wells up out of the cuts and runs down, and in one drop a black thorn turns into view. Above the cuts, a huge eyelid of bark twitches.

**Loop:** Amber drips, the green light flickers, moonlit leaves fall, the eyelid twitches.

**Sound:** A knife scrape for each cut, a wood crack, a drip.

### 4. The ambush

**English:** When the Elves see Lethiel with you, they think that you took the First Gardener prisoner. They block the road and call the beasts out of the caves.

**Indonesian:** Saat para Elf melihat Lethiel bersamamu, mereka mengira kamu menawan sang Tukang Kebun Pertama. Mereka menutup jalan dan memanggil binatang-binatang buas keluar dari gua.

**Shot:** The mossy glade of the Battle Painting. An Elf horn sounds from the treetops, and rings of sound shake the leaves. Thorn hedges whip shut across the road. Eyes open in the dark cave mouth at the lower left. A Bristleback Boar charges out of the cave, straight at the camera. It smashes through a line of three wooden stakes, one behind the other: the first Trample of the Region. At 3.1 s it fills the frame, and the camera shakes. At the upper right, a squirrel holds a stolen Elf arrow.

**Loop:** The boar snorts steam, dust settles, leaves fall, the hedge sways.

**Sound:** A horn, a whip of thorns, a boar squeal, three wooden crashes, a heavy impact.

### 5. Your road

**English:** Fight your way to the Bramble Hill. The amber that paid Glimmerbough came from the giant's wounds, and you must learn who cut them.

**Indonesian:** Bertarunglah sampai ke Bramble Hill. Amber yang membayar Glimmerbough berasal dari luka sang raksasa, dan kamu harus mencari tahu siapa yang menyayatnya.

**Shot:** The map in afternoon light. A thorny vine of golden light grows along the Trail from the Leaning Gate, and each of the ten clearings blooms with a ring of leaves as it passes. With each clearing, a tremor runs through the forest, and the Old Bramble's eye opens a little more. At the end, the vine reaches the Bramble Hill, and the eye flares amber.

**Loop:** Light flows along the vine, the clearings breathe, the giant's eye glows.

**Sound:** A rising chime and a soft thud for each clearing, a deep groan at the hill.

## Boss

The Boss scene plays when the Player selects Fight on Stage 4-10 for the first time. It is a conversation: the Old Bramble talks, and you answer. Each Story Moment has one to three lines. Continue shows the next line; the clip changes only at the next moment. After the last line, Fight starts the Battle. Skip also starts the Battle.

The Old Bramble is huge, slow and very old. It speaks in short, heavy sentences, and it calls the Hero "small one". Its motive is pain and anger: small ones cut its bark every night, and the battles woke it. It thinks that the Hero is one more cutter. The Hero shows it the amber to explain, and the amber looks like proof of the theft. The joke of the Region is that the Hero's best clue makes things worse.

In the lines below, `Old Bramble:` and `You:` are speaker labels. The game shows them as name plates, with the Hero portrait for `You:`. A line with no label is narration.

### 1. The giant wakes

**English:** The noise of your battles wakes the Old Bramble. The whole hill shakes, and two great eyes open in the bark. / Old Bramble: Who... cuts... my bark?

**Indonesian:** Suara pertempuranmu membangunkan Old Bramble. Seluruh bukit berguncang, dan dua mata besar terbuka di kulit kayunya. / Old Bramble: Siapa... yang menyayat... kulit kayuku?

**Shot:** The map. The camera pushes in on the Bramble Hill while the ground shakes. Flocks of birds burst out of the trees, the brambles on the hill writhe, and leaves and dust rain down. Two eyes open in the bark of the painted face, and amber light pours out of them. A ring of sound runs out from the face.

**Loop:** Leaves fall, the eyes glow and blink slowly, small tremors, birds circle.

**Sound:** A rumble, bird wings, a deep groan, a roar.

### 2. The Old Bramble

**English:** You: I did not cut you. I came to find the ones who did. / Old Bramble: Small ones always say that. Small ones with small knives.

**Indonesian:** You: Bukan aku yang menyayatmu. Aku datang untuk mencari pelakunya. / Old Bramble: Makhluk kecil selalu berkata begitu. Makhluk kecil dengan pisau kecil.

**Shot:** A huge close-up of the face in the bark: brows of bark, a beard of moss and a crown of red leaves. The bark of the mouth cracks open with splinters, the eyes narrow, and amber light glows inside them. The name plate THE OLD BRAMBLE grows letter by letter above a thorny bramble.

**Loop:** The moss beard sways, leaves fall from the crown, the eyes glow, the mouth breathes.

**Sound:** A deep groan, wood cracks, a low hit for the name.

### 3. The cutters

**English:** Old Bramble: Every night, they come with cold green fire. They cut my bark and carry my amber away. / Old Bramble: Every cut wakes me a little more. Now I am awake, and I am angry. / You: Then they did not want only your amber. They wanted you awake.

**Indonesian:** Old Bramble: Setiap malam mereka datang membawa api hijau yang dingin. Mereka menyayat kulit kayuku dan membawa pergi amberku. / Old Bramble: Setiap sayatan membuatku sedikit lebih terjaga. Sekarang aku terjaga, dan aku marah. / You: Kalau begitu, mereka tidak hanya menginginkan ambermu. Mereka ingin kamu bangun.

**Shot:** The giant's memory: roots in deep blue moonlight, seen from the side. Small hooded figures walk between the roots in a line, each with a swinging lantern of cold green fire. They stop at the bark, raise their knives, and the cuts flash. Amber spills into their jars. Above them, a huge eye in the bark opens a little more with each cut, and the roots tremble.

**Loop:** The green lanterns sway, amber drips, the eye glows, moonlit mist drifts.

**Sound:** Soft steps, a knife scrape, a wood crack, a low groan.

### 4. The thorn in your hand

**English:** You: Your amber paid for glowcaps in the Glowcap Hollows. I followed it here. / Old Bramble: My amber, with my thorn. And it is in your hand, thief!

**Indonesian:** You: Ambermu dipakai untuk membayar glowcap di Glowcap Hollows. Aku mengikutinya sampai ke sini. / Old Bramble: Amberku, dengan duriku. Dan benda itu ada di tanganmu, pencuri!

**Shot:** One drop of amber rises into the foreground, backlit, with the black thorn inside. Behind it, the giant's eye fills the background, out of focus and the same gold as the drop. The eye focuses on the drop. Its lids of bark slam down into an angry slit, and red leaves fall.

**Loop:** Light flows in the drop, the angry eye glows and quivers, leaves fall.

**Sound:** A shimmer for the drop, a deep groan, a wood crack as the eye narrows.

### 5. The challenge

**English:** Old Bramble: Enough! Colossus, stand up! Brambles, close the first lane! / You: Then we fight, old giant. But I am not the one with the knife.

**Indonesian:** Old Bramble: Cukup! Colossus, berdirilah! Semak duri, tutup jalur pertama! / You: Kalau begitu kita bertarung, raksasa tua. Tapi bukan aku yang membawa pisau.

**Shot:** The mossy glade of the Battle Painting. Three lanes of golden light race across. A Bramble Nest card slams into the first Lane, and thorny brambles burst up around it. The ground of the third Lane cracks, rocks rise, and the Mountain Colossus card rises out of the rubble with a ring of dust. The giant's eyes flare at the right. This is the real start of the Boss Battle: a Bramble Nest in Lane 1 and a Mountain Colossus in Lane 3.

**Loop:** Energy flows along the lanes, the brambles sway, dust drifts, the eyes glow.

**Sound:** War drums, a card slam, a rock crash, a roar.

## Ending

### 1. The giant rests

**English:** The Old Bramble sits down with a sound like a hundred falling trees. Its eyes grow heavy, and the beasts creep back into their caves.

**Indonesian:** Old Bramble duduk dengan suara seperti seratus pohon tumbang. Matanya menjadi berat, dan binatang-binatang buas merangkak kembali ke gua mereka.

**Shot:** The drawn face of the giant in warm late light. It sinks down with a heavy jolt, and dust and leaves rain down. Its lids droop slowly, and the amber light inside them dims. At the bottom of the frame, beast eyes in the dark cave mouths blink and go out one by one. A long breath blows leaves out of its beard.

**Loop:** Leaves fall, the half-closed eyes glow faintly, its breath stirs the moss, motes drift.

**Sound:** A deep thud, a long groan, falling leaves, a soft chime.

### 2. The First Gardener

**English:** Lethiel touches the cuts with a glowing staff, and Holy light closes them. The Thornwood Elves lower their bows. / Lethiel: A fine old tree. Adequate.

**Indonesian:** Lethiel menyentuh sayatan-sayatan itu dengan tongkat yang bercahaya, dan cahaya Holy menutupnya. Para Elf Thornwood menurunkan busur mereka. / Lethiel: Pohon tua yang bagus. Cukup.

**Shot:** The giant's bark of Opening 3, now in warm daylight, with three cuts and dried amber. The glowing tip of Lethiel's staff moves into the frame and touches each cut. A seam of golden light runs along it, the bark knits closed, and small leaves sprout from the seam.

**Loop:** The healed seams glow, the leaves sway, golden motes rise.

**Sound:** A shimmer for each cut, a soft chime.

### 3. The empty camp

**English:** Under the roots, you find the cutters' empty camp. They left their pay behind: coins of bone, cold as ice, in a purse with the seal of three lanes, cut through.

**Indonesian:** Di bawah akar, kamu menemukan perkemahan para penyayat yang kosong. Mereka meninggalkan upah mereka: koin-koin tulang, sedingin es, di dalam kantong bersegel tiga jalur yang terpotong.

**Shot:** A dark hollow under giant roots. A fallen lantern of green fire gutters out, and warm daylight falls in. Jars of amber and the cutters' knives glint. A purse tips over, and coins of bone spill out and roll. One coin wobbles to a stop in front of the camera, and frost creeps over it. The wax seal of the purse burns violet.

**Loop:** Cold mist rises from the coins, the frost glitters, the seal glows, dust drifts in the light.

**Sound:** A purse, a rattle of bone coins, a low hit for the seal.

### 4. The Colossus

**English:** Before its eyes close, the Old Bramble sends the Mountain Colossus with you. / Old Bramble: Find the ones with the knives, small one. My Colossus will walk with you.

**Indonesian:** Sebelum matanya terpejam, Old Bramble mengirim Raksasa Gunung bersamamu. / Old Bramble: Temukan mereka yang membawa pisau, makhluk kecil. Colossus-ku akan berjalan bersamamu.

**Shot:** Rocks roll together and stack up, and the Mountain Colossus card art forms out of them. A frame of stone and moss draws itself around the card with the Epic glow. A flock of small birds lands on the card's top edge.

**Loop:** Dust drifts, the frame glows, the birds hop, moss sways.

**Sound:** Rolling rocks, a heavy thud, a bright shimmer, bird wings.

### 5. The Hollow Marches

**English:** Coins of bone are paid in one place only: the Hollow Marches, where a dead ferryman takes travelers over the water. The trail goes on.

**Indonesian:** Koin tulang hanya dipakai di satu tempat: Hollow Marches, tempat seorang tukang perahu yang sudah mati menyeberangkan para pengelana. Jejaknya berlanjut.

**Shot:** One coin of bone spins up in front of the camera. The camera flies through the hole in its center, and the Hollow Marches Region Map appears in cold mist. The Bone Ferry glides on the water, the green fire bowls of the Witchfire Bridge flare one after another, frost glitters on the hills, and the cut seal burns faintly in the mist.

**Loop:** Mist drifts, the green fires flicker, the frost glitters, the seal breathes.

**Sound:** A coin ring, a deep whoosh, a cold wind, the unanswered phrase of the theme.

## Player checks

- Can the Player say why the Hero is in the Thornwood after the opening?
- Can the Player say why the Elves fight the Hero, and why the Old Bramble is angry?
- Can the Player say what the cutters wanted, and why they would enter the Hollow Marches after the ending?
- Does the Player feel sympathy for the Old Bramble after the Battle?
- Does the seal still make the Player curious, without a claim about who the enemy is?
- Do the moments feel worth watching, and is Skip easy to find?
